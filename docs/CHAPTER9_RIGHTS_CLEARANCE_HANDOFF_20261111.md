## Permission scopes to establish, separately

1. **Independent review:** whether protected textbook passages and any figures may be disclosed to a specified external generative-AI or independent review provider, including processing/retention terms. Local instructor-controlled source transformation does **not** grant this.
2. **App reuse/publication:** whether derivative learning activities, relevant textbook expression, questions or diagrams may be distributed on a publicly accessible learning app, with any territory, educational/noncommercial, translation, derivative-work, display, attribution, fee, expiry or user-access limitations.
3. **Third-party figures:** the original publisher's rights may not cover every embedded photograph, diagram, illustration or embedded licensed image. Establish the rightsholder/credit and permission for every asset actually reused, or replace with wholly original independently created visual work under a **separately admitted** pilot/content scope.
4. **Source and version binding:** a permission grant must identify the actual title/author/edition/section or relevant licensed assets and the permitted operations. Preserve any grant privately; only a redacted authority/date/scope/identity/digest may be recorded in public Git.
5. **Separate non-rights obligations:** final EN/MS/ZH physics and visual meaning review, authorized learner-pilot/privacy evidence, and explicit release approval remain necessary even if rights are granted.

**Explicit stop:** until an independently verifiable grant/license covers the applicable scope, `sourceAndFigureRights` stays `NOT_VERIFIED`, `physicsAnswersAndTrilingualMeaning` and `diagramVisualPedagogy` stay `NOT_VERIFIED`, and publication stays `NOT_AUTHORIZED`. ## Official permission routes identified (public source research on 11 Nov 2026)

- **If, and only if, the correct work is identified as Cengage-controlled:** Cengage directs reuse requests to the Copyright Clearance Center first, then to its official rights form if the title is not available. It explicitly treats educator and publisher reuse as a permission request, *not* an automatic right: https://www.cengagegroup.com/permissions/ and https://permissions.cengage.com/permissions/action/step1 .
- **Alternative open textbook is not an automatic substitution:** OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1*, Chapter 9 is now described on its official live pages as **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, with mandatory attribution and share-alike terms. The current preface says its content must **not** be ingested into generative-AI offerings without OpenStax's prior written permission. Some older PDF files still show a different historical license. Treat the actual version's governing terms and any third-party attribution as requiring fresh verification before using it. See https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/preface and https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/9-introduction .
- Do not treat an OpenStax or other rights-cleared alternative as evidence for the **existing six frozen package hashes**. A genuinely new pilot needs an independently justified, explicitly authorized, separately reviewed scope under Decision 0032.
