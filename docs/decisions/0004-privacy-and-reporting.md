# 0004 — Privacy and reporting

Status: Accepted, 15 August 2026

- Track first attempts, retries, time, hints, success, progression, and revisits.
- Teacher priorities: mastery by student, misconception patterns, attempts/time, participation, and completion.
- Student priorities: mastery map, learning/XP history, and independent versus assisted evidence.
- Raw audio is deleted immediately after transcription.
- Tutor transcripts are temporary; compact summaries follow an approved retention schedule.
- Detailed read-only supporter access requires learner consent, revocation, auditability, and institutional review.
