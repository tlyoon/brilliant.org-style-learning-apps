# 0002 — Learning, mastery, and motivation

Status: Accepted, 15 August 2026

- Each publishable subchapter contains 18 activities: nine MCQ and nine interactive, evenly split across easy, moderate, and challenging difficulty.
- Activities are conceptual and calculator-free.
- Named mastery level is primary; percentage is secondary.
- Cohort standing is subchapter-specific and requires adequate comparable evidence.
- First-attempt performance is separate from assisted improvement.
- XP, restrained achievements, and learning streaks are allowed.
- Limited lives and high-pressure global leaderboards are excluded.
