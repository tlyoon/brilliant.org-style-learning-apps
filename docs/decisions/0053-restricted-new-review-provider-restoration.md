The recovery adds one extremely narrow, **verified historical quarantine and new-request probation lane**, not a general ignore-unknown switch:

1. The recovery selector itself invokes the existing BSA-190 exact-parent/content remedy and refuses a mismatch, altered old role, stale receipt, captured old response, changed source or orphaned reviewer evidence.

Its optional internal `quarantined_historical_claim=(exact-original-package-SHA, exact-claim-file-SHA)` omits **at most one** verified *unclassified old* request from the *new activity provider capacity* status only. The first restricted live trial is limited to ONE still-untouched original Section9.6 input with new immutable source-bound SHA, after authorized-host check, paid/transfer charter check, shared original/supplemental provider and PRE_CALL preflights, an authenticated source-free `models.get` health check, and the normal exact one-activity two-Gemini independent reviewer.

Zero synthetic science decisions count for owner QA.

Across restarts the recovery recalculates from immutable actual source-bound supplemental evidence. No background job or unlimited recurrence is created.
