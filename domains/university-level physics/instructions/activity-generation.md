# University-level physics activity-generation extension

Generate original conceptual physics activities whose answer logic follows from the controlled source corpus plus approved prerequisites. Prefer prediction, comparison, classification, ordering, graph/diagram reasoning, misconception diagnosis, and qualitative parameter exploration over disguised calculation. Use the physical system and assumptions consistently across prompt, options, hints, feedback, and visual intent.

Do not invent forces, conservation conditions, coordinate conventions, signs, trajectories, apparatus, or interactions merely to make an activity easier to render. When a visual or simulation materially improves reasoning, request a supported physics representation through the visual-planning contract.
